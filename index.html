Biology · How Real Ants Optimize

When foraging, ants initially wander at random. Upon finding food they return to the nest while laying a trail of pheromone. Other ants detect the chemical and are more likely to follow stronger trails. Because shorter paths are completed faster, more pheromone accumulates on them before evaporation acts. Over time the colony converges on the shortest route — a distributed, adaptive optimization process known as swarm intelligence.

Help & Concepts

What is TSP?

The Traveling Salesman Problem asks for the shortest closed tour that visits each city exactly once and returns to the start. It is NP-hard; exact solutions become impractical beyond a few dozen cities, so metaheuristics such as ACO are used.

What is ACO?

Ant Colony Optimization is a population-based metaheuristic inspired by the foraging behaviour of ants. Artificial ants construct solutions probabilistically using pheromone trails and heuristic information, then update the trails according to solution quality.

What is Biomimicry?

Biomimicry is the practice of learning from and then emulating natural forms, processes and ecosystems to solve human design challenges. ACO is a classic example of biomimetic computing.
